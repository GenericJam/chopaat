# Adopting Mob into chopaat (plain mix lib → runnable Mob app)

- Date: 2026-08-30
- Bead: `chopaat-mgw`
- Status: accepted

## Context

The repo was a pure rules-engine library. The game-screens bead makes it a
runnable Mob app. mob_dev ships `mix mob.adopt.*` sub-tasks for exactly
this, so we used them — where they let us.

## Decision

**Hybrid adoption.** mob_dev's guarded sub-tasks (`mob.adopt.deps`,
`mob.adopt.mob_app`, `mob.adopt.mob_exs`, `mob.adopt.screen`) refuse any
project without `:phoenix` in deps — a deliberate pre-1.0 policy
("widen the guard, never the silent-proceed path",
mob_dev `decisions/2026-06-19-mob-adopt-lives-in-mob_dev.md`), not an
oversight, and `mob.adopt.deps` additionally re-emits mix.exs from AST,
destroying comments. So:

- **Generated by task:** `android/` and `ios/` native trees via
  `mix mob.adopt.native` (unguarded by design; templates from the
  mob_new checkout, `MOB_BUNDLE_PREFIX=com.genericjam` to keep
  `mix mob.uninstall --all-apps` blast radius away from `com.example.*`).
  Untouched afterwards; template drift is detect-don't-autopatch
  (`mix mob.doctor`).
- **Hand-written to the tasks' canonical shapes** (verbatim from
  `MobDev.Adopt.Patcher`'s thin-client variant, adjusted only where
  noted): `lib/chopaat/mob_app.ex` (root screen is our MenuScreen, not
  the WebView `MobScreen`; sets the mob_scene3d asset root at `on_start`
  because device BEAMs don't load Mix config), `src/chopaat.erl`,
  `mob.exs` (plus `config :mob, :plugins, [:mob_scene3d]`, which adopt's
  `mob.exs` subset omits), and the mix.exs edits (`erlc_paths`,
  `erlc_options`, deps) — preserving the file's comments.
- **Preserved by construction:** existing `lib/`, `test/`, `priv/`,
  `assets/` — the native installer's globs are platform-scoped and
  everything else was hand-edited. Verified: the full pre-existing suite
  (102 tests) passes unchanged.

Deps: `{:mob, "~> 0.7.37"}` (hex), `{:mob_dev, "~> 0.6.30", only: :dev}`
(0.6.29's hex package could not compile), `{:igniter, "~> 0.8"}` (the
native task's runtime), and **mob_scene3d as a path dep** pointing at the
local master checkout (`MOB_SCENE3D_PATH` overrides; CI clones the public
repo) until the animation lane releases — the integration bead
(`chopaat-hre`) pins the hex release.

Candidate upstream gap (mob_dev tracker, not filed from here): a
supported adopt path for plain non-Phoenix mix projects, so the three
hand-written files stop being hand-written.

## Consequences

- `mix setup` activates `.githooks` (two-tier pre-push copied from
  mob_scene3d); CI (`.github/workflows/test.yml`) runs the mix gates and
  the asset gate (`assets/scripts/gate.mjs`).
- The app entry is `Chopaat.MobApp` → `Chopaat.Screens.MenuScreen`;
  nothing starts OTP-app-wise on device beyond what `on_start/0` does.
